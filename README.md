# NeuroGuard Open

### Document. Understand. Protect Human Agency.

**NeuroGuard Open** is an open-source research and civic-technology project designed to help researchers, journalists, civil society organizations, digital-rights groups, and the public document, study, review, and report potentially manipulative AI-generated and AI-assisted content.

As AI-generated content becomes easier to create and distribute, understanding its potential influence requires more than simply asking whether something was generated by AI.

NeuroGuard focuses on **evidence, context, uncertainty, human review, and research**.

---

## What is NeuroGuard?

NeuroGuard Open is being developed as an open research infrastructure for documenting emerging forms of AI-enabled influence.

The platform helps users:

* Document potentially concerning content
* Preserve relevant context and sources
* Identify potential influence or manipulation techniques
* Organize supporting evidence
* Record uncertainty and alternative interpretations
* Enable community review
* Connect cases with research and references
* Generate structured case reports
* Build reusable open research datasets

**NeuroGuard does not automatically determine that content is manipulative.**

The platform is designed to support human investigation and research rather than replace human judgment.

---

## Why NeuroGuard?

Traditional AI-content detection often focuses on questions such as:

> "Was this content generated by AI?"

NeuroGuard explores a different question:

> **"What can we document and understand about how AI-generated or AI-assisted content may influence people?"**

AI generation alone does not establish manipulation.

A piece of content may be AI-generated without being harmful, deceptive, or manipulative. Conversely, influence can involve context, targeting, distribution, intent, personalization, or presentation that cannot be established from the content alone.

NeuroGuard therefore emphasizes:

**Evidence → Context → Human Review → Research**

rather than automated conclusions.

---

## How It Works

### Document → Understand → Review → Research → Report

**1. Document**

Capture a case, source, platform, content type, date, and relevant context.

**2. Understand**

Identify observable characteristics and potential techniques that may warrant further investigation.

**3. Review**

Allow researchers and community contributors to examine evidence, challenge interpretations, and identify missing information.

**4. Research**

Connect documented cases with research papers, frameworks, datasets, and related cases.

**5. Report**

Generate structured case reports that preserve evidence, uncertainty, sources, and analysis.

## Core Platform

NeuroGuard Open is being developed around several connected components.

### Case Library

A searchable collection of documented cases with information such as:

* Case ID
* Content type
* Platform
* Date
* Potential techniques
* Evidence
* Review status
* Sources
* Related research

### Case Investigation

A structured environment for examining an individual case.

Each investigation can include:

* What was observed
* Supporting evidence
* Potential interpretation
* Alternative interpretation
* Open questions
* Evidence timeline
* Community review
* Related research
* Sources

### Evidence Framework

NeuroGuard is designed to make uncertainty visible.

Instead of presenting an automated binary judgment, cases can document:

* Available evidence
* Missing evidence
* Source reliability considerations
* Conflicting information
* Alternative explanations
* Questions requiring further investigation

### Manipulation & Influence Taxonomy

The project includes a developing research taxonomy covering areas such as:

* Emotional Influence
* Personalized Persuasion
* Behavioral Targeting
* Deceptive Content
* Synthetic Media
* Hidden Influence
* Context Manipulation

This taxonomy is a **working research framework**, not a definitive classification system.

## Responsible AI Principles

NeuroGuard Open is guided by several principles:

### Evidence Before Conclusions

Document observable evidence before making interpretations.

### AI Generation ≠ Manipulation

The fact that content was generated or assisted by AI does not automatically establish manipulation.

### Uncertainty Should Be Visible

Cases should communicate what is known, what is uncertain, and what remains unresolved.

### Human Judgment Matters

Consequential judgments about intent, manipulation, harm, or meaning should remain subject to human review.

### Alternative Interpretations

Investigations should allow competing or alternative explanations where evidence is incomplete.

### Transparent Methodology

Research frameworks, taxonomies, and methodologies should be documented openly.

### Responsible Data Practices

The project should minimize unnecessary personal data collection and consider privacy, security, and potential harms associated with public case documentation.

### Open Research

Where appropriate, datasets, methodologies, documentation, and tools should be reusable by researchers and civil society organizations.

## Research Question

NeuroGuard Open explores the following research question:

> **How can open, evidence-based infrastructure help researchers identify and study emerging forms of AI-enabled influence without automating consequential judgments about intent?**

This question will evolve as the project develops and as researchers and contributors provide feedback.

---

## Why Open Source?

The societal effects of AI-enabled influence should not be studied only through closed systems.

An open approach can help researchers and civil society organizations:

* Examine methodologies
* Reproduce research
* Contribute new cases
* Challenge assumptions
* Improve taxonomies
* Build independent tools
* Share research resources
* Develop common evidence standards

NeuroGuard Open is intended to become a collaborative research infrastructure rather than a closed detection product.

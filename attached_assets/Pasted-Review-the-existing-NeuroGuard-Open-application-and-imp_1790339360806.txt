"NeuroGuard does not automatically determine whether content is manipulative. It helps humans document evidence, identify potential techniques, investigate cases, and conduct review."

* Potential Technique
* Under Community Review
* Evidence
* Observation
* Analysis
* Open Question
* Needs More Evidence

Avoid:

* Manipulation Detected
* Dangerous Content
* AI Verdict

Do not imply that NeuroGuard can determine intent.

# 5. ADD A METHODOLOGY PAGE

This is a HIGH PRIORITY.

Add "Methodology" to the navigation and footer.

Create a professional methodology page.

Sections:

## What NeuroGuard Documents

Explain that cases may contain:

* content
* source
* platform
* date
* context
* evidence
* potential techniques
* research notes
* review status
* related research

## Observation vs Interpretation

Explain the distinction.

Example:

Observation:
"The video contains synthetic visual elements."

Interpretation:
"The content may have been generated or modified using AI."

Do not present interpretation as fact unless evidence supports it.

## What NeuroGuard Does Not Determine

Include:

* It does not determine creator intent.
* It does not equate AI-generated content with manipulation.
* It does not automatically establish wrongdoing.
* It does not replace human review.
* A taxonomy label is not proof of manipulation.

## Evidence & Uncertainty

Explain how evidence is recorded and how incomplete evidence is represented.

## Community Review

Explain how researchers/contributors can review cases and add notes.

Make this page feel like actual research methodology rather than marketing copy.

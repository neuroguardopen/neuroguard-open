# NeuroGuard Open — Methodology

## Purpose

NeuroGuard Open is designed to support the documentation and study of potentially manipulative AI-generated and AI-assisted content.

The methodology prioritizes evidence, context, transparency, uncertainty, and human review.

NeuroGuard is not intended to automatically determine whether a piece of content is manipulative.

---

## Core Principle

The central methodological principle is:

> **Document evidence before drawing conclusions.**

A case may contain content that appears unusual, persuasive, misleading, emotionally targeted, or AI-generated. These observations can justify further investigation, but they do not automatically establish intent, manipulation, deception, or harm.

---

## Investigation Workflow

NeuroGuard organizes investigations into five stages:

### 1. Document

Record the available information about a case.

This may include:

* Case identifier
* Content
* Content type
* Platform
* Source
* Date discovered
* URL or reference
* Description
* Relevant surrounding context

The goal is to preserve what was observed without immediately assigning a conclusion.

---

### 2. Understand

Identify observable characteristics that may warrant further investigation.

Examples may include:

* Emotional targeting
* Personalized persuasion
* Behavioral targeting
* Synthetic media
* Misleading context
* Hidden sponsorship or identity
* Potentially deceptive presentation

These are documented as **potential techniques or characteristics**, not definitive findings.

---

### 3. Review

Evidence should be reviewed by people rather than relying solely on automated classification.

Reviewers may examine:

* Supporting evidence
* Source reliability
* Context
* Alternative explanations
* Missing information
* Contradictory evidence
* Questions requiring further investigation

Different reviewers may reach different interpretations. The system should preserve meaningful disagreement rather than hide it.

---

### 4. Research

Cases can be connected to:

* Academic research
* Existing frameworks
* Related documented cases
* Datasets
* Methodological resources
* Relevant reporting
* Other public sources

This allows individual cases to contribute to a broader research ecosystem.

---

### 5. Report

A case can be converted into a structured report containing:

* What was observed
* Available evidence
* Potential interpretations
* Alternative interpretations
* Review history
* Related research
* Sources
* Remaining uncertainties

Reports should distinguish observations from interpretations.

---

# Evidence Framework

NeuroGuard encourages contributors to distinguish between:

### Observation

What can directly be seen, heard, documented, or otherwise established from available material.

### Interpretation

A possible explanation of what the observed evidence may indicate.

### Supporting Evidence

Information that strengthens a particular interpretation.

### Alternative Interpretation

Another plausible explanation that should be considered.

### Open Question

Information that is currently unavailable or requires additional investigation.

This structure is intended to reduce premature conclusions.

---

# Uncertainty

Uncertainty is a normal part of research.

A case may have:

* Strong evidence for some observations
* Limited evidence for other claims
* Conflicting sources
* Unknown provenance
* Unclear intent
* Incomplete context

NeuroGuard should make these limitations visible rather than presenting false certainty.

---

# Human Review

NeuroGuard is designed around human review for consequential interpretations.

Human reviewers may:

* Add evidence
* Challenge interpretations
* Suggest alternative explanations
* Identify missing context
* Correct factual errors
* Link additional research
* Recommend further investigation

Community review should function as a research and evidence-review mechanism rather than a popularity system.

---

# AI-Assisted Analysis

AI may eventually assist with tasks such as:

* Organizing information
* Extracting structured fields
* Identifying potentially relevant research
* Summarizing evidence
* Suggesting questions for investigation
* Detecting inconsistencies for human review

However, AI-generated suggestions should remain distinguishable from documented evidence.

Automated suggestions should not be treated as proof of manipulation, intent, deception, or harm.

---

# Data and Privacy

Case documentation may contain sensitive information.

The project should therefore follow responsible data practices, including:

* Collecting only information necessary for the research purpose
* Avoiding unnecessary personal information
* Considering the risks of publishing identifiable information
* Documenting sources responsibly
* Respecting applicable laws and ethical research standards
* Providing mechanisms for correcting factual errors where appropriate

Public case documentation should consider potential harm to individuals and communities.

---

# Reproducibility

Where practical, NeuroGuard aims to make research processes understandable and reproducible.

This includes documenting:

* Methodology
* Taxonomy definitions
* Data structures
* Review processes
* Research sources
* Changes to frameworks

Open documentation allows researchers and contributors to examine and improve the project's approach.

---

# Limitations

NeuroGuard Open is an early-stage research project.

The platform does not currently provide a validated system for:

* Determining intent
* Proving manipulation
* Determining whether a person is malicious
* Establishing psychological effects
* Predicting how an audience will respond
* Automatically determining whether content is harmful

These questions generally require context, evidence, domain expertise, and human judgment.

---

# Future Methodological Development

As the project develops, the methodology may explore:

* More structured evidence provenance
* Inter-reviewer agreement
* Case comparison frameworks
* Dataset documentation
* Multilingual research
* Source reliability frameworks
* Reproducible case analysis
* Responsible AI-assisted research tools

Changes to the methodology should be documented openly as the project evolves.

---

## Guiding Principle

> **NeuroGuard does not aim to replace human judgment. It aims to give people better infrastructure for exercising it.**

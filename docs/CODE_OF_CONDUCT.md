# NeuroGuard Open — Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

NeuroGuard Open is intended to be a collaborative space for research, technology, journalism, civil society, and public-interest work.

We are committed to creating an environment where people can contribute respectfully, regardless of their background, experience level, or technical role.

---

## Expected Behavior

Contributors are expected to:

* Treat others with respect.
* Discuss ideas and evidence constructively.
* Welcome different perspectives.
* Give credit to other contributors.
* Ask questions when information is unclear.
* Distinguish disagreement with an idea from criticism of a person.
* Respect privacy and confidentiality.
* Keep research discussions focused on evidence and methodology.

---

## Research and Discussion Standards

NeuroGuard deals with topics that can involve contested evidence and interpretation.

Contributors should therefore:

* Clearly distinguish observations from interpretations.
* Identify sources where possible.
* Avoid presenting unverified claims as established facts.
* Acknowledge uncertainty.
* Consider reasonable alternative explanations.
* Avoid unnecessary personal information.
* Avoid targeting individuals through project discussions.

Disagreement is acceptable and expected.

Personal attacks are not.

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

* Harassment
* Personal attacks
* Threats or intimidation
* Discrimination
* Deliberate misinformation about contributors
* Publishing private information without permission
* Doxxing
* Hate speech
* Sexual harassment
* Malicious disruption of project infrastructure
* Attempts to manipulate research records or evidence

---

## Reporting Concerns

If you experience or observe unacceptable behavior, please report it privately to the project maintainers.

For security vulnerabilities, please follow the instructions in `SECURITY.md` rather than publicly disclosing the vulnerability.

Reports should include enough information for the maintainers to understand the concern and investigate it appropriately.

---

## Maintainer Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for:

* Reviewing reported concerns.
* Protecting contributors from harassment.
* Applying project standards consistently.
* Keeping discussions focused on constructive research and development.
* Taking appropriate action when community standards are violated.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

* GitHub repositories
* Issues
* Pull requests
* Project discussions
* Community research activities
* Official NeuroGuard community spaces

---

## Final Principle

NeuroGuard Open aims to create infrastructure for better understanding of complex questions.

That requires a community where people can challenge evidence, question assumptions, and disagree with interpretations without attacking one another.

**Challenge the evidence. Improve the method. Respect the people.**

# NeuroGuard Open — Roadmap

## Project Direction

NeuroGuard Open is being developed as open-source research infrastructure for documenting and studying AI-enabled influence.

The project will evolve gradually from a working prototype into a community-supported research platform.

---

## Phase 1 — Working Prototype

**Current focus**

The initial prototype establishes the core research workflow:

**Document → Understand → Review → Research → Report**

### Current capabilities

* Case documentation
* Public case library
* Case investigation interface
* Evidence documentation
* Potential technique taxonomy
* Community review concept
* Research references
* Structured case data
* Responsible AI methodology
* Open-source project documentation

### Current status

**Early-stage open-source prototype**

The current system is intended for experimentation, research, and community feedback rather than production deployment.

# Research Priorities

Development will prioritize research usefulness over feature volume.

The project will focus on:

### Evidence

Improving how evidence is collected, preserved, connected, and reviewed.

### Context

Helping researchers understand content within its broader distribution and social context.

### Taxonomy

Developing a practical and evolving framework for describing potential influence techniques.

### Human Review

Keeping consequential interpretation and judgment transparent and human-led.

### Open Data

Creating reusable datasets and resources that can support independent research.

### Reproducibility

Making research processes understandable and repeatable where ethically and legally possible.

---

# What We Are Not Building First

To keep the project focused, early development will not prioritize:

* Automated manipulation scores
* Fully automated intent detection
* Black-box risk classifications
* Social-media engagement features
* Advertising systems
* Complex commercial dashboards
* Automated decisions about individuals

The project is designed around evidence and human judgment rather than automated labeling.

---

# Near-Term Milestones

The immediate development priorities are:

1. Strengthen the public case library.
2. Improve the investigation interface.
3. Add structured evidence workflows.
4. Connect cases to the taxonomy.
5. Publish example research cases.
6. Improve contribution documentation.
7. Establish an initial open dataset structure.
8. Document the methodology and limitations.
9. Gather feedback from researchers and civil society practitioners.
10. Iterate the platform based on research and community feedback.

---

# Long-Term Vision

NeuroGuard Open aims to become a reusable open-source foundation for studying how AI-generated and AI-assisted content can influence people.

The long-term objective is not to create a system that decides what people should believe.

It is to create infrastructure that helps people:

**Document what happened.**

**Understand the available evidence.**

**Consider different interpretations.**

**Review findings with others.**

**Conduct research.**

**Make better-informed judgments.**

---

## Guiding Principle

> Build infrastructure for human understanding, not automated judgment.
